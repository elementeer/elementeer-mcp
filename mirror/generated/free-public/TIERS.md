## Pricing Logic

Elementeer multipliziert Elementor — es ersetzt es nicht:

| Combination | Surpasses |
|------------|-----------|
| Elementeer Free + Elementor Free | Elementor Essential (paid) |
| Elementeer Advanced + Elementor Free | Elementor Advanced |
| Elementeer Advanced + Elementor Advanced | Elementor Expert |
| Elementeer Studio + Elementor One | Full agentic control |

Individual modules (Stock Images, AI Generation, Governance Pro, Multi-Site) are available as granular add-ons — no all-or-nothing upgrade.

---

## What Free Already Provides (That Elementor Essential Does Not)

- `assess_site` — Full site health report (Elementor: not available)
- `get_recommendations` — Context-aware action plan (Elementor: not available)
- `set_global_colors` / `set_global_typography` — Automated, not just manual
- `creator_mode` — Template composition from existing library by brief
- `wizard_brand_setup` — Coordinated brand setup in one step
- Multi-site support (Elementor requires separate licenses)
- `compose_page_from_templates` — Layout assembly without the editor

---

## What Advanced Adds

- Full Creator Mode AI integration (automatic section generation)
- Theme Builder Wizard (auto-build header/footer from library)
- Stock Images integration (Pexels/Unsplash API)
- Governance Profiles for agencies
- Change Review Queue (AI proposes, human approves)
- AI Image Generation (integrated, no separate service needed)
- Complete agentic site management (posts, media, users)
- White-label ready for agencies
- Priority support + SLA

## Studio Future

Studio is the planned cloud orchestration layer — not yet available:

- Cross-site delivery pipelines
- Conversational orchestration
